- `user/source-pack/`: 37 files from the September 12, 2026 intake, including two #86 papers, selected material from two pinned repositories, a forum observation, and two machine-learning papers cited in the lectures.

Only selected lecture-page text and the original PDF hash were imported; the complete supplied lecture PDF remains outside this repository. Interpretations are in the [research overview](../docs/existing-research.md) and [machine-learning methods note](../docs/ml-methods-from-lectures.md).

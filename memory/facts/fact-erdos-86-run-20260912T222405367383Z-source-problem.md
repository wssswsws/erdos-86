## statement

Let $Q_n$ have vertex set $\{0,1\}^n$, with two vertices adjacent when they differ in exactly one coordinate. Write $f(n)=\operatorname{ex}(Q_n,C_4)$ for the maximum number of edges in a subgraph containing no cycle of length four. The original problem asks whether $f(n)=(\tfrac12+o(1))n2^{n-1}$ as $n\to\infty$. Our initial finite research target is to understand $C_4$-free subgraphs of $Q_7$ with 304 edges and search for a verifiable construction with at least 305 edges, or obtain a precisely scoped structural or exclusion result.

\section*{Erdos Problem 86: research scope}
\begin{problem}
Let $Q_n$ have vertex set $\{0,1\}^n$, with two vertices adjacent when they
differ in exactly one coordinate. Write $f(n)=\operatorname{ex}(Q_n,C_4)$
for the maximum number of edges in a subgraph containing no cycle of length
four. The original problem asks whether
$f(n)=(\tfrac12+o(1))n2^{n-1}$ as $n\to\infty$.
Our initial finite research target is to understand $C_4$-free subgraphs of
$Q_7$ with 304 edges and search for a verifiable construction with at least
305 edges, or obtain a precisely scoped structural or exclusion result.
\end{problem}

\section*{Known baseline and limits}
Known constructions have 304 edges in $Q_7$ and 682 edges in $Q_8$.
Within the odd-square subclass, where every square retains exactly one or
three edges, the maxima are 304 and 682 respectively. An improvement must
therefore leave that subclass. The unrestricted optimal values in these
dimensions are not settled by those subclass results.

A finite improvement does not resolve the original asymptotic problem.
Failure of a heuristic search is not an upper-bound proof. Exhaustive
exclusion under additional restrictions must retain those restrictions
in its statement. Reproducing a known construction is baseline validation,
not a claim of a new result.

\section*{Evidence requirements}
Record full edge lists, source versions, search parameters, and verification
commands. Check that all edges belong to $Q_n$, that there are no duplicates,
and that every square has at most three selected edges. Independently check
that no two vertices have two common neighbors. Proof claims require an
explicit argument or independently checkable exact certificates.
